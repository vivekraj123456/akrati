Case Study 3 · Anjana’s Glam Hub
A makeup artist’s Instagram, built from zero
01
Starting point
A Bhopal makeup artist with a brand-new page: 0 followers, 0 reach, no content and no online presence for her bridal, engagement and glam work.
02
What I did
Planned and ran the page end to end: profile and bio set-up, visual identity, a monthly content plan, and every reel, post and story, from the studio’s “Opening soon” launch to bridal looks and festive posts.
03
Impact
From zero to 2,025 followers, with a consistent, recognisable presence that showcases her work to local brides and clients.
